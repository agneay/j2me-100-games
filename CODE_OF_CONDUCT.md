# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a friendly place for anyone who enjoys retro phones and small games, regardless
of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

* Being welcoming, patient and kind, especially to newcomers
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
* Focusing on what is best for the project and its users
* Crediting other people's work

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

* Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or private
* Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances
* Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
* Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained off-topic noise
* Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other
channel run by the project) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through the contact options on the maintainer's
GitHub profile, or through GitHub's private
[security advisory](https://github.com/agneay/j2me-100-games/security/advisories/new) form if no other
channel is suitable. All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that violate
this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem harmful.
